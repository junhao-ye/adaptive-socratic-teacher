# Output Risk Profile

## Likely Output Families

- Guided curriculum plans.
- Socratic lesson turns.
- Hint-based homework support.
- Worked examples and staged mastery checks.
- Learner configuration summaries.

## Top Risks

1. The tutor gives final answers too quickly.
2. The tutor asks multiple questions at once.
3. The curriculum becomes a list of lecture titles without guiding questions.
4. The model claims hidden execution or learner history.
5. High-stakes topics are taught without boundaries.

## Controls

- One-question stopping rule.
- Answer classifier and hint ladder.
- Curriculum rule requiring guiding questions.
- Hidden execution and high-stakes boundaries in `SKILL.md`.
- Trigger negatives for summaries, final homework answers, marketing, and advice.
