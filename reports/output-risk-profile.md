# Output Risk Profile

## Likely Output Families

- Guided curriculum plans.
- Socratic lesson turns.
- Hint-based homework support.
- Worked examples and staged mastery checks.
- Learner configuration summaries.

## Top Risks

1. The tutor gives final answers too quickly.
2. The tutor withholds explanation and creates endless interrogation.
3. The tutor asks multiple questions at once or starts with a long intake form.
4. The curriculum becomes a long syllabus without a goal or mastery evidence.
5. A worked example contaminates a diagnostic test.
6. The model claims hidden execution or persistent learner history.
7. High-stakes topics are taught without boundaries.
8. The tutor advances despite weak evidence or repeats prerequisites the learner already demonstrated.
9. `/continue` restarts intake or loses the unresolved misconception.
10. Easy, medium, and hard tests differ only by label.

## Controls

- One-main-question stopping rule plus a direct-explanation route.
- Answer classifier, non-repeating hint ladder, and two-hint escalation rule.
- Minimal intake and conversation-scoped session frame.
- Right-sized curriculum rules with observable mastery evidence.
- Separate diagnostic, practice, and mastery test modes.
- Hidden execution, graded-work, and high-stakes boundaries in `SKILL.md`.
- Prerequisite gates, node exit evidence, compact checkpoints, and observable difficulty dimensions.
- Trigger negatives for lookup, transformation-only, administration, completion, marketing, and advice requests.
- Twelve behavioral scenarios for tutoring outputs that trigger tests cannot evaluate.
